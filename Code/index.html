潮起高崎，文化共鸣

项目名称 厦门高崎海洋文化时尚艺术季
设计时间 2025.05-2025.12
项目地点 中国福建厦门高崎社
功能类型 文化艺术策展
主策划 熊晓龙、王思琦

厦门城市设计周高崎海洋文化时尚艺术季是依托高崎寨历史文脉、海洋文化和城市更新实践开展的综合性公共艺术活动，也是2025年厦门城市设计周分会场活动之一。

The Gaoqi Ocean Culture and Fashion Art Season of Xiamen Urban Design Week is a comprehensive public art activity based on the historical context of Gaoqi Village, ocean culture, and urban renewal practices. It is also one of the branch venue activities of Xiamen Urban Design Week 2025.

活动以"海·潮"为主题，通过19处公共空间艺术装置、开幕活动、主题沙龙、成果展示、导览打卡和公众互动等形式，联动政府、高校、社区、专家、艺术家与设计机构共同参与，探索以公共艺术激活社区空间、传播城市形象、提升公共文化服务的新路径。作为策展团队主策划，负责艺术节从立项策划、场地选点、方案生成到施工落地的全过程统筹。项目涉及政府部门、街道、社区、19家设计单位、2家施工单位及灯光设计团队等多方协作，我负责组织跨主体沟通、推进方案修改、协调现场搭建与活动执行，保障空间装置、文创系统、开幕式及沙龙活动按期落地，提升高崎社公共空间品质与文化传播影响力。

Themed "Ocean·Tide", the event features 19 public space art installations, opening ceremonies, themed salons, achievement exhibitions, guided tours, and public interactions. It brings together governments, universities, communities, experts, artists, and design institutions to explore new paths for activating community spaces, spreading urban images, and enhancing public cultural services through public art. As the chief curator of the curatorial team, I was responsible for the overall coordination of the art festival, covering the full process from project initiation, curatorial planning, site selection, and proposal development to construction implementation. The project involved multi-party collaboration among government departments, subdistrict offices, communities, 19 design firms, 2 construction teams, and the lighting design team. I was responsible for organizing cross-stakeholder communication, advancing proposal revisions, coordinating on-site construction and event execution, ensuring the timely delivery of spatial installations, cultural and creative systems, the opening ceremony, and salon events, while enhancing the quality of Gaoqi Community’s public space and its cultural communication influence.

晋江松熹中学改扩建设计

项目名称晋江松熹中学改扩建设计
时间2023-2024
状态在建
类型学校项目
面积41719㎡
团队姚敏峰、台文彬、熊晓龙、杨文豪

晋江市松熹中学改扩建工程位于晋江市安海镇庵前村，基地中部有庵前溪穿过，自然形成南、北两个地块，其中北地块整体较为方正，南地块呈梯形。

The renovation and expansion project of Songxi Middle School in Jinjiang is located in Anqian Village, Anhai Town, Jinjiang. Anqian Creek runs through the center of the site, naturally dividing it into northern and southern plots. The northern plot is relatively regular in shape, while the southern plot is trapezoidal.

在设计理念上，本项目以“为学生和教师创造良好的学习与成长空间”为核心目标。设计希望在有限的校园用地条件下，通过合理的空间组织，营造丰富的校园活动场所，形成兼具学习、交流、活动与文化体验的校园环境。建筑布局注重教学空间之间的联系与互动，强调灵活、多元的学习场景，为学生提供开放而有活力的成长空间，也为教师提供高效、舒适的教学与办公环境。整体设计延续原有校园格局，并结合改扩建需求，对功能空间进行优化整合。通过新建、改建与保留建筑的有机结合，形成疏密有致、联系紧密的校园空间结构，在满足教学、生活、活动等功能需求的同时，塑造具有秩序感和文化氛围的现代校园形象。

In terms of design concept, the project takes “creating a high-quality learning and growth environment for students and teachers” as its core objective. Within the limited campus site, the design organizes spaces efficiently to create diverse areas for campus activities and to form an educational environment that integrates learning, communication, activity, and cultural experience. The architectural layout emphasizes the connection and interaction between teaching spaces, supporting flexible and diverse learning scenarios. It provides students with an open and dynamic environment for growth, while also offering teachers an efficient and comfortable space for teaching and office work. The overall design continues the existing campus structure and optimizes the functional spaces according to the renovation and expansion requirements. Through the integration of new construction, renovation, and retained buildings, the project forms a campus spatial structure with balanced density and close connectivity, meeting the needs of teaching, daily life, and activities while shaping a modern campus image with order and cultural atmosphere.

福建厦门大清一等邮局

项目名称福建厦门大清一等邮局
时间2025
状态方案
类型室内设计
团队姚敏峰、熊晓龙、魏嘉

厦门一等邮局，光绪二十二年（1896年），清政府正式开办大清邮政，一年后，厦门邮政成立，时称“大清厦门邮政局”，设厦门邮界总局。宣统三年（1911年），厦门邮政总局改称“厦门一等邮局”，是福建省惟一的一个“一等甲级邮局”。

Xiamen First-Class Post Office traces its history to the 22nd year of the Guangxu reign, 1896, when the Qing government officially established the Imperial Chinese Post. One year later, the Xiamen postal service was founded under the name ‘Imperial Qing Xiamen Post Office,’ with the Xiamen postal district headquarters established. In the third year of the Xuantong reign, 1911, the Xiamen General Post Office was renamed ‘Xiamen First-Class Post Office,’ becoming the only first-class Grade A post office in Fujian Province.

此次设计以“大清一等邮局”的历史记忆为线索，将近代邮政符号转译为空间语言。通过延续拱券窗、复古绿、花砖、木质柜台与暖色灯光等元素，营造兼具年代感与亲和力的室内氛围。空间植入邮票展陈、书信记忆、文创售卖、集章互动与研学沙龙等功能，使旧邮局从单一历史场所转化为可游览、可停留、可传播的城市文化体验空间。

This design takes the historical memory of the Imperial Qing First-Class Post Office as its narrative thread, translating modern postal symbols into spatial language. By extending elements such as arched windows, vintage green tones, patterned tiles, wooden counters, and warm lighting, the design creates an interior atmosphere that combines a sense of history with approachability. Functions such as stamp exhibitions, letter-memory displays, cultural and creative retail, stamp-collection interaction, and educational salons are introduced, transforming the old post office from a single historical site into an urban cultural experience space that can be visited, stayed in, and shared.

本科作品集以公共建筑与社区空间更新为主线，收录了社区活动空间、校园图书馆与社区文化活动中心三个阶段性设计。作品从“乡愁记忆”“学习场景重构”与“场所精神”等议题出发，关注建筑如何回应人的日常行为、公共交往与城市记忆。通过场地调研、概念生成、功能组织、形体推演、图纸表达与效果呈现，作品集展现了我在本科阶段对空间叙事、公共性营造、场所更新及建筑综合设计能力的持续探索。

This undergraduate portfolio focuses on public architecture and the renewal of community spaces, including three phased design projects: a community activity space, a campus library, and a community cultural activity center. Starting from themes such as ‘nostalgic memory,’ ‘reconstruction of learning scenarios,’ and ‘spirit of place,’ the works explore how architecture responds to everyday behavior, public interaction, and urban memory. Through site research, concept development, functional organization, form generation, drawing expression, and visual presentation, the portfolio demonstrates my continuous exploration of spatial narrative, publicness, place renewal, and comprehensive architectural design during my undergraduate studies.
